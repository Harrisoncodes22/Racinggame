# Velocity Cup — Home

Arcade kart-style racer for Roblox: a floating **server room** hub, waiting rooms for friends, races against bots or players on 6 maps, items, drifting, a coin shop, Robux game passes, a daily spin and per-track fastest-lap leaderboards.
